IO Home Lab

A private assistant for a connected home.

IO is a privately operated home-lab system that helps manage household services and media requests.

What IO does

When enabled by its owner, IO can process messages sent to a configured Gmail mailbox, identify requests from approved senders, and carry out supported home-lab workflows. Depending on the request, IO may organize messages, prepare an unsent reply draft, or send a configured confirmation or status update.

IO is a personal system. It does not offer public accounts or accept sign-ups.
